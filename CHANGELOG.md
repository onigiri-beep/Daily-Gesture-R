# Changelog

## [0.1.15-beta]

# **English:**
🎨 Daily Gesture — What's new?

## Architecture & Performance

* **Multiplatform:** New codebase with native support for Windows, Linux, and Android.
* **Optimization:** Significant improvements in transition performance and image loading.

## **Interface**

* **Ghost Interface:** 100% clean main screen, focused exclusively on drawing practice.
    * Sessions screen now hides everything to improve your concentration. Click to make everything appear again!
* **Settings Drawer:** All utilities are now located in the newly added settings screen.

## User Experience (UX)

* **Silent Badge:** Discreet notification system for updates (red dot), with no invasive pop-ups when starting the app.
* **More Languages:** Full real-time support for English, Portuguese (BR), and Spanish.
* **Integrated History:** New remote Changelog viewer to track improvements directly in the app.

## **Training Features**

* **Expanded Capacity:** The limit for references per session has been increased from 10 to 20 images.
* **Folder Limit:** The limit for folders loaded in the menu has been increased from 3 to 9.
* **Effects:** Added Grayscale effect during sessions.
* **Always on Top (desktops only):** Added button to overlay other apps while you train, preventing others from covering it 📌.
* **Flip H and V:** Added flip to turn the image Horizontally (H) and Vertically (V) during the session.
